About Accurate Info Solution

Building Technology Around Real Business Needs

Accurate Info Solution was founded in 2018 in Bengaluru with a clear intent: deliver practical, innovative technology that works inside real business environments. We design and build systems around how organizations actually operate their processes, data, constraints and people.

Since then the company has evolved across software development, AI and Machine Learning, Web and Mobile Technologies, IoT, Embedded Systems, Robotics and Business systems bringing these capabilities together as end-to-end engagements rather than isolated projects.

SAP Solutions — IT Services

SAP: Our Dedicated IT Service

SAP is a dedicated IT services practice at Accurate Info Solution — implementation, integration, optimization and ongoing technology support delivered as a standalone engagement. We work inside your existing SAP landscape, align it to how your business actually runs, and keep it stable, performant and supported.

Engagements are scoped independently: no bundled products, no mandatory add-ons. Where an organization later wants intelligence on top of its business data, that is a separate service track.
